# Dashboard walkthrough

One page for a Princeton official. Say only what is below. The figures are the ones in [municipal-briefing.md](municipal-briefing.md). On the project machine, from `/agent/repos/CEE420/princeton-flood-accessibility`, run `python3 scripts/dashboard.py`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/. The page is on that machine. It is not a public website.

**Banner.** Read it aloud. The map is two hypothetical water surfaces on a USGS 3DEP bare-earth clip: +1.0 m and +2.0 m above a nearby mapped channel. They are separate assumptions. They are not hours, there is no time slider, and they were not scaled by El Niño. They are not Hurricane Ida on 1–2 September 2021. The cuts 0.10 m, 0.30 m, and the half-length fraction are preliminary assumptions, not a closure standard. The streets are the OpenStreetMap drive network: 1,298 centerlines, 232,984.8 m inside the town.

**+1.0 m, then +2.0 m.** Switch the two buttons. Do not call them hours. Green is passable, yellow is the 0.10–0.30 m band, orange is partly at or above 0.30 m but still routed, red is removed because at least half the street is that deep, purple is an unknown deck, and gray is unmatched, not certified dry. The screen rounds this run to 8.3 km wet and 3.7 km removed (33 streets) at +1.0 m, and 19.2 km wet and 14.3 km removed (90 streets) at +2.0 m. If someone wants the unrounded counts: 8,307.0 m wet and 3,732.3 m removed at +1.0 m; 19,159.8 m wet and 14,327.2 m removed at +2.0 m. Streets with a deep sample somewhere, including streets that stay open, are 143 and 234. Connected pieces go from 13 to 42. Most of those new pieces are single leftover nodes. The largest piece goes from 228.6 km (913 nodes) to 173.7 km (754 nodes). Hospital routes stay connected at +1.0 m and disconnect at +2.0 m. The hospital in that check is Princeton House Behavioral Health. Zero of 39 traffic-count records sits on a removed street. Those counts were not added up.

**Quaker Road.** Click the long purple way. News did record a closure: Planet Princeton and Princeton Patch on 2 September 2021, a sinkhole note on 3 September from Mercer Street to the West Windsor border, no reopening estimate on 8 September, the canal-bridge collapse in Town Topics on 20 October, and reopening reported on 22 December. The scenario does not reproduce that. Ten centerlines are named Quaker Road. None is removed at either assumption, and the deep-piece length on that name is 0 m. The long bridge way is 2,826.5 m, County Route 533. It matches National Bridge Inventory structures 1100082 (Quaker Road over Stony Brook, 24.7 m, built 2013) and 3000154 (Quaker Road over the Delaware & Raritan Canal, 14.6 m, built 1951, reconstructed 2020). Neither has a published deck elevation. Purple means the deck is unknown. It does not mean the road was checked and found dry. Do not read 99.99 feet, the Route 206 gage datum of 61.35 feet, or a “floods at 9 feet” note as the deck.

**FEMA checkbox.** It is at the top right, labeled **Regulatory flood zones**, and it starts on. Read the line under it: “Regulatory. Effective 20 July 2016. Not Hurricane Ida. Not a scenario depth.” The same date is in the legend and the side panel. These polygons are the effective flood map. They do not change the red and green roads. Uncheck the box and the polygons leave; check it and they return. Centerline length inside the zones is 3,952.4 m in the AE floodway, 5,916.6 m in AE, 383.3 m in A, 2,105.6 m in the 0.2% zone, and 220,627.8 m in minimal zone X. A zone letter is not a depth. The 65.5 feet NAVD 88 figure is the regulatory base-flood water surface at one Stony Brook cross section. It is not the Quaker Road deck and it was not copied onto a road. Minimal zone X is not a measured dry street.

**September 2021 news reports.** The checkbox under the flood-zone control is off until you check it. Black dashes then mark Quaker Road, Mercer Road, Stockton Street, South Harrison Street, and Rosedale Road. The dashes are the news reports. They are not a model depth, and they do not replace the green, yellow, orange, or red road color. Click a dash and read the news date. Quaker Road, Stockton Street, South Harrison Street, and Rosedale Road: the scenario did not close the road. Mercer Road: the scenario did not close it at +1.0 m, and it assumed 2 of 14 centerlines closed at +2.0 m. Rosedale Road was reported open again at about 4 p.m. on 2 September 2021. That sentence is not a closure log. South Harrison Street has a wording gap: Patch named South Harrison Street, and Planet Princeton named Harrison Street without the word South.
